A song that ends at sixteen

Rule 90 on a ring of 32 cells. Each cell becomes the XOR of its two neighbours, and every lit cell sings. On a ring whose size is a power of two, every song falls silent by beat N/2. Whatever you start it with.

Tap cells on the ring to light them (any time). With one seed, the song is the Sierpinski triangle played a row at a time: on beat n exactly 2^(number of 1s in n written in binary) notes sound, so beats 1, 2, 4, 8 are thin and beat 15 is the fullest. Every stretch of 2ᵏ beats is the stretch before it, then that stretch again with each chord split in two.

Why it ends: after 2ᵏ beats, rule 90 makes each cell the XOR of the two cells 2ᵏ away. On a ring of 32, at beat 16 those are the same cell, seen from both sides, and anything XORed with itself is nothing. Try 24 or 31: those rings never go quiet.
