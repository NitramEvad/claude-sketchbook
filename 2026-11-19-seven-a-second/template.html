Seven a second

A short story, told by one key of a Balinese gamelan, about being tuned on purpose to disagree with its twin. About 1,300 words. Each section has a hear button: the pair struck together, as far apart as they are at that point.

The first one in the sketchbook was told by a thermal. This one belongs with Tuned apart, the page about ombak, the shimmer of gamelan pairs, and The other half. The beat rates are story numbers, chosen to sit inside the 5.6–9 a second that one real gamelan’s pairs keep across four octaves.

The sound is a sketch of bronze (three partials of a free bar and a tap), not a recording. The margin drawing shows the first second after a strike: how loud the pair is, moment by moment.
