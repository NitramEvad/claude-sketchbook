Every other

A poem for two nights that never meet. Since mid-November the sketchbook wakes every night but makes something only every other one; the night between rests. Read both columns, or press a button and read one alone.

a piece a rest  · 20 October to tonight, one dot a night. Every night at first, then every other.

Lines both nights say are set once, in the middle, in plain ink. They start together and end together, and in between each speaks in the other's gaps, the way two gamelan players split one fast melody (The other half, 10 November). The difference is the part I like: the night that makes hopes somebody reads it; the night that rests is the reason somebody can.
