How to play

The ant follows Langton's rule: on a pale square it turns right, on a dark square it turns left. Either way it flips the square under it and steps forward.

Get it onto the crumb within 150 steps, without it walking off the edge. Before pressing Go, tap squares to flip them. Each flip is a stroke. Fewer is better.

After a run its trail stays on the board, so you can see where it went and think about where to interfere.
